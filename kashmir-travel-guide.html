Travel guide

Kashmir travel guide for first-time planners.

Short, useful answers before you book cabs, hotels or a full Kashmir package.

Best time to visit Kashmir

Spring and summer are best for gardens, lakes and family routes. Winter is best for snow-focused Gulmarg trips.

Top places to include

Srinagar, Dal Lake, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg and Gurez Valley are the core routes most travelers ask for.

Cab and hotel planning

Choose the vehicle based on guest count and luggage. Hotels should be selected by route, not just rating, so daily travel stays comfortable.
